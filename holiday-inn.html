Holiday Inn

Location
Mobile, Alabama
Type
Hospitality
Size
17 stories
Status
Completed

Renovation of an existing 17-story hotel in Mobile's downtown historic district, within walking distance of the city's museums, science center and waterfront.

The work brings the hotel into conformance with current Holiday Inn brand standards while adapting them to an older building, whose existing structure, ceiling heights and floor plates set firm limits. Public spaces, from the reception lobby and lounge to the elevator halls, were reworked with a warm, contemporary palette, new lighting, finishes and furnishings, meeting the brand’s requirements and giving a familiar presence on Mobile’s skyline a renewed sense of arrival.
