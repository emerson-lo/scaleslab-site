A creative coworking office in Gardena, with private studios along a bright corridor and shared work areas in saturated color.

The existing strip mall shell, with its timber trusses and concrete floor, was kept and left exposed. Paint, plywood and glass do most of the work: colored walls define the zones, built-in plywood counters provide work surfaces and storage, and glass-fronted studios share daylight with the corridor.
